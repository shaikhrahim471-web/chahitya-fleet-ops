Roadmap

No driver ever has to log in or use an app. Here's what changes as GPS hardware is added — same screens, better data underneath.

Now — manager-tap model
Live today, needs no new hardware.
EventHow it's captured
Driver present todayManager marks login/logout once a day
Trip departs loading siteManager taps "Mark departed" — one tap, no typing
Trip arrives/dumps at dump groundEstimated — inferred from the tanker's next departure
Idle-tanker flagTime since last "departed" tap exceeds normal cycle time
Later — GPS-confirmed model
Once trackers are installed on tankers (separate hardware decision, not yet started).
EventHow it's captured
Driver present todaySame — manager marks login/logout
Trip departs loading siteAuto-detected by geofence — manager tap becomes a cross-check, not the source
Trip arrives/dumps at dump groundExact — geofence-confirmed timestamp, no inference
Idle-tanker flagReal stationary-time detection, anywhere on the route — not just at the yard
Why this order
The manager-tap model needs no new hardware, no driver app, and no budget approval to start — it's ready as soon as this system is built. GPS adds precision (exact dump-site timestamps, full-route idle detection) but is a separate hardware/vendor decision with recurring cost, parked for later per our earlier discussion. Nothing built now gets thrown away when GPS arrives — it's an upgrade to where one column's data comes from, not a different system.
Accounts & access
No public sign-up, no Google/social login — this stays a closed, admin-issued system to match a small operation. The owner creates a username + password for each person individually (one login per Director, one per Manager, not shared credentials) and hands it to them directly. There's no self-serve "forgot password" — a forgotten password means asking the owner to reissue one. Nothing to build for this on the Director/Manager screens; it's handled at setup, not as a feature either of them sees.
